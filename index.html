The artist

John-Mark Sammut

Maltese artist, drawing since secondary school. Specialises in pencil work, portraits and freehand design — the tattoo starts on paper, in the studio's sketchbooks.

  • Early years — falls in love with drawing at school; pencil never leaves the pocket.
  • Australia — two years abroad, one and a half of them working in a tattoo shop.
  • February 2017 — back home to Malta, starts tattooing full time and opens D'n'A in Bugibba.
  • 2025 — featured artist at the International Malta Tattoo Show.
  • Beyond skin — also a commissioned illustrator; his art covers a Maltese band's latest EP.
